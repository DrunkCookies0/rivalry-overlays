Getting caster cameras on screen, two ways:
Remote casters / dedicated producer: use VDO.Ninja below. Set one room, click Generate, and send each caster their personal link. Their camera shows up framed on the Casters overlay automatically, no accounts or software for them to install.
Solo (you cast and produce): open your own generated link in a browser tab to feed your camera, or add your webcam in OBS as a Video Capture Device source placed over the Casters overlay.
Set a room + caster names, then Generate: it auto-fills each caster's feed below and gives you a push link to send each caster. (Or paste view links manually.)
